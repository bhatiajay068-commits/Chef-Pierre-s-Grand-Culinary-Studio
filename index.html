🍰 How Recipe Rescaling Works in Our Cafe

1. Find The Scale Factor

Divide the servings you want by the servings the recipe makes. For example, 8 wanted ÷ 4 made = 2.0x factor.

2. Multiply Each Ingredient

Multiply every ingredient's original quantity by that scale factor so proportions remain harmonious.
